# Brand identity assets

Logos are stored locally under public/brand-logos; catalog: public/brand-logos.json.
Asset source: https://github.com/filippofilip95/car-logos-dataset (thumb images; original sources credited in its logos/data.json).
Logos identify the selected vehicle manufacturer; trademarks remain their owners’ property.

Verified campaign lines (wording and use can vary by region):

- BMW: https://www.bmw-welt.com/en/ausstellungen/ausstellungen-im-bmw-museum/haus-der-marke.html
- Mercedes-Benz: https://www.mbusa.com/content/mb-nafta/en_us/best-or-nothing.html
- Audi: https://www.audi.com/en/technology-235
- Porsche: https://www.porsche.com/usa/there-is-no-substitute/
- Toyota: https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-reveals-new-tagline-lets-go-places/
- Ford: https://www.fromtheroad.ford.com/us/en/articles/2023/new-ford-_built-ford-proud-campaign-celebrates-the-next-generati
- Lexus: https://www.lexus.ca/en/discover/lexus-brand/

Brands without a verified line show their logo and name only. Unknown names hide the identity strip. Theme palette families are not used as identity aliases: e.g. Honda never receives Toyota's logo or slogan.
